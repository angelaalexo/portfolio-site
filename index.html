A little about me

I build things because I'm curious about what happens between an idea and the moment it actually works.

My projects usually start with one question: “Could I build that?” From there, I like figuring out everything in between — designing the interface, structuring the data, building the backend, experimenting with AI, or debugging the thing that refuses to cooperate.

I've explored software engineering, web development, databases, AI, and 3D, and I enjoy working where technology meets creativity. I care about both how something is built and how it feels to use.

I'm comfortable being stuck because that's usually where I learn the most. My approach is simple: build → break → understand → improve.

I'm still early in my career, and that's what excites me — there's always something new to build, learn, and make better.
